# Awesome-ERP

## Top ERP Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Enterprise Resource Planning, Financials, Inventory, Manufacturing, Supply Chain, CRM Integration & Business Process Management*

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)**. These systems integrate core business processes — finance, inventory, manufacturing, sales, purchasing, HR, and more — into a unified platform for end-to-end operational visibility and control.



**Examples** include SAP S/4HANA, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, Odoo, Acumatica, Sage Intacct, Epicor ERP, IFS Cloud, Infor CloudSuite, and SYSPRO (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project. Odoo Community, ERPNext, Dolibarr, Tryton, Apache OFBiz, and related systems provide powerful, fully open alternatives for SMBs and mid-market organizations seeking data ownership and lower total cost of ownership.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[SAP S/4HANA](https://www.sap.com/products/erp/s4hana.html)**  

  Flagship enterprise ERP from SAP, offering real-time in-memory processing, deep industry functionality, and global scalability for large and complex organizations.



- **[Oracle NetSuite](https://www.netsuite.com/)**  

  Leading cloud ERP suite covering financials, inventory, order management, CRM, and e-commerce, widely adopted by growing mid-market and multi-subsidiary companies.



- **[Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics-365/products/business-central)**  

  Cloud ERP tightly integrated with the Microsoft ecosystem (Office 365, Power Platform, Teams), popular with SMBs and mid-market organizations.



- **[Odoo (Enterprise)](https://www.odoo.com/)**  

  Modular business suite (open-source core + paid enterprise apps) covering CRM, accounting, inventory, manufacturing, e-commerce, and more.



- **[Acumatica](https://www.acumatica.com/)**  

  Cloud-native mid-market ERP known for flexible licensing, strong manufacturing and distribution capabilities, and consumption-based pricing options.



- **[Sage Intacct](https://www.sage.com/en-us/sage-intacct/)**  

  Best-in-class cloud financial management and accounting platform, often used as a core financials system or paired with operational ERPs.



- **[Epicor ERP (Kinetic)](https://www.epicor.com/)**  

  Industry-focused ERP strong in manufacturing, distribution, and supply chain, with deep vertical functionality.



- **[IFS Cloud](https://www.ifs.com/)**  

  Enterprise ERP and asset management platform particularly strong in asset-intensive industries, service, and project-centric businesses.



- **[Infor CloudSuite](https://www.infor.com/)**  

  Industry-specialized cloud ERP suites covering manufacturing, distribution, healthcare, and other verticals.



- **[SYSPRO](https://www.syspro.com/)**  

  ERP solution focused on manufacturers and distributors, offering strong inventory, production, and supply-chain capabilities.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Odoo Community](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  The most widely used open-source ERP/business suite, with modules for accounting, inventory, manufacturing, CRM, e-commerce, HR, and extensive community apps (OCA).



- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Fully open-source, modern ERP built on the Frappe framework, covering accounting, inventory, manufacturing, CRM, HR, projects, and industry-specific needs.



- **[Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)**  

  Lightweight, modular open-source ERP & CRM designed for small and medium businesses, freelancers, and foundations.



- **[Tryton](https://github.com/tryton)**  

  Highly modular, three-tier open-source ERP framework (Python) with a clean architecture and strong accounting foundations.



- **[Apache OFBiz](https://github.com/apache/ofbiz)**  

  Apache Software Foundation project providing a comprehensive suite of business applications and a framework for building custom ERP solutions.



- **[iDempiere](https://github.com/idempiere/idempiere)**  

  Open-source ERP + CRM based on the ADempiere/Compiere lineage, featuring OSGi modularity and active community development.



- **[Metasfresh](https://github.com/metasfresh/metasfresh)**  

  Open-source ERP focused on manufacturing, distribution, and wholesale, with modern web interfaces.



- **[ADempiere](https://github.com/adempiere/adempiere)**  

  Mature open-source ERP derived from Compiere, still maintained by its community for specific use cases.



- **[LedgerSMB](https://github.com/ledgersmb/LedgerSMB)**  

  Double-entry accounting and light ERP system oriented toward small businesses and bookkeeping needs.



- **[xTuple / PostBooks and related forks](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source manufacturing and distribution ERP options that remain available in community form.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Axelor Open Suite and other low-code / open ERP frameworks.

- Industry-specific modules and localizations maintained by Odoo Community Association (OCA) and ERPNext community.

- Accounting-focused open-source tools that can serve as financial cores.

- Custom extensions built on Frappe, Odoo, or Tryton frameworks.

- Integration layers connecting open ERPs to e-commerce, payment, and logistics systems.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Start with **Odoo Community** or **ERPNext** for the broadest functional coverage and community support. Use **Dolibarr** for lighter SMB needs, **Tryton** or **Apache OFBiz** when architectural cleanliness or extreme modularity is required, and **iDempiere/Metasfresh** for organizations already familiar with the Compiere lineage. Self-host on your infrastructure, extend with community modules, and integrate via standard APIs or middleware. Many companies run these systems in production for years with full data ownership and no per-user license fees.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- ERP implementations are complex, high-stakes projects involving process redesign, data migration, and change management. Open-source solutions eliminate license costs but still require skilled implementation partners, ongoing maintenance, and careful evaluation of functional fit, localization, and support options.

- Always perform thorough due diligence, proof-of-concept testing, and total-cost-of-ownership analysis before selecting any ERP platform.
